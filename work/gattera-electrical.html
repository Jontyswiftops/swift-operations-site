Our work · Case study

Gattera Electrical: one system from the first quote request to tax time

Jarid Gattera is a licensed electrician working across the Sunshine Coast and South East Queensland. He wanted customers to find him, request a quote without phone tag, and have the jobs, paperwork and receipts all in one place. We built it from scratch, on his own domain, with no extra software subscription.

What we built

Four parts, working as one system

01

A website that brings in work

A fast, mobile-first site with its own pages for the suburbs Jarid services, including Beerwah, Caloundra, Landsborough and Maleny, plus a page for smoke alarm installation. Click to call throughout, and a link to his Google reviews.

02

Online quote requests

Customers describe the job on their phone in a couple of minutes, with photos, suburb, postcode, property type, urgency and the best time to call. It lands in Jarid's system complete, not as a missed call.

03

The Job Book

Every job in one place. Quotes with line items that customers accept in one tap, booking confirmations, day-before reminders, job notes with photos, and a weekly summary by email.

04

Expenses and tax time

Snap a receipt and it is logged with the GST. Money in against money out over six months, reminders before licences and insurance expire, and a tax time summary for this year and last.

For his customers

Three steps, no phone tag

  1. Tell us about the job online

    A short form on their phone, with photos of the job and the postcode, so Jarid knows what he is walking into.

  2. The quote lands in their inbox

    Jarid calls back, then sends a written quote by email. The customer accepts it with one tap.

  3. Booked in, reminded, done

    A booking confirmation, a reminder the day before, and a receipt when it is paid. All of it sends on its own.

For Jarid

The back office, sorted

  • Every job, quote and booking in one place, on his phone or laptop.
  • Quotes built from line items and accepted by the customer in one tap.
  • Confirmations and reminders that send themselves.
  • Job notes and photos kept against the job.
  • Safety records for pre-start checks and high-risk work.
  • Smoke alarm compliance certificates, produced from the job.
  • Receipts, GST and a tax time summary ready for the accountant.

How it is built

The whole back office runs on Google, so there is no monthly software fee on top. The website sits on his own domain.

Everything lives in his accounts and belongs to him. It was built for one fixed price, and it is his to keep.

“Very happy with the outcome. It has made quoting and following up leads that much easier, with simple clicks of buttons and one place to view all my data. I wanted something that was easy to navigate and work with, as I found I was spending too much time manually doing everything beforehand.”

Jarid Gattera, Gattera Electrical Services
