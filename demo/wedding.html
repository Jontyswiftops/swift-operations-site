Our story

Six years, two dogs, one question

We met at a mate's barbecue in 2021, when Tom burnt the sausages and Mia offered to take over the tongs. She has been in charge of the barbecue ever since.

Since then we have moved house twice, adopted Biscuit and Pepper, and driven most of the east coast in a very old campervan. Last winter, on a walk to our favourite lookout, Tom finally asked. Mia said yes before he got the ring out.

We can't wait to celebrate with the people we love most. Thank you for being part of our story.

Questions

Good to know

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation includes a guest, their name will be on it. Pop them in your RSVP so we can save them a seat.

Are kids welcome?

We love your little ones, but this one is a grown-ups' celebration. Babes in arms are the exception.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking on the lawn by the gate. If you plan to enjoy a drink, the shuttle is the way to go.

What if I have dietary needs?

Let us know in your RSVP and we'll sort it with the caterers.

Will the ceremony be inside if it rains?

If the weather turns, the ceremony moves to the barn. We'll update this page on the morning.
