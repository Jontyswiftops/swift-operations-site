What counts as a request

One defined change or fix: a workflow, a field or layout change, a report, an import or dedupe, a form or app connection, a user setup, an email template, or a monthly hygiene check. Anything bigger than about a day of work we flag before starting and break into requests where it makes sense.

The rules

Everything through the dashboard, not phone or email. Acknowledged the same business day. Broken things started within 4 business hours, Monday to Friday. Three-month minimum, then monthly with 30 days notice. Unused requests do not roll over. We take an export before any bulk change and log every change on the request.
