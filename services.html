How a project runs

From first chat to handover

1. Consultation and proposal

A free consultation to understand how you work. Then a written proposal: exactly what we will build, what is not included, the timeline, and one fixed price. You accept it with one click.

2. Deposit and your project page

Pay the 50% deposit and we book in the build. You get your own project page to follow progress, send questions and files, and see what we need from you.

3. Build and review

We build it in your systems, then walk you through it. Changes within the agreed scope are part of the price. Anything outside it we quote before doing.

4. Sign off and handover

When you are happy, you sign off on your project page. The final 50% invoice comes through, and once it is paid the system is yours, with aftercare starting straight away.

Pricing and aftercare

Fixed price, never hourly

Every project is a fixed price for a defined result. Most systems land between $2,000 and $15,000 ex GST depending on how much is involved. Half to start, half at handover once you are happy with it.

Aftercare

  • 7 days of fixes included after handover. If anything we built breaks, we fix it at no charge.
  • 30-day aftercare is optional, priced with your project. Add it when you sign off.
  • Aftercare covers genuine breaks in what we built, reported on your project page.
  • New features, changes to how it works, or a new system are quoted separately. So are problems caused by changes someone else makes or by your software provider.

The rules

  • The proposal sets the scope. Changes inside it are included; anything outside it is quoted first.
  • Nothing is charged by the hour.
  • Your tools, your accounts. Everything we build lives in your systems and stays yours.
  • We take a backup before any bulk change, and every change is written down in plain English.
