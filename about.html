About Swift Operations

Less admin. More of the work that matters.

Swift Operations was founded to solve a simple problem. Businesses of all sizes are spending too much time on admin and not enough time doing the work that actually matters.

We see the same pattern everywhere. A business is juggling five or six apps, a few spreadsheets, and a system or two that were set up years ago and never revisited. Data gets copied from one place to another by hand. Follow-ups depend on someone remembering. Hours disappear every week into repetitive tasks that add nothing. And most of the time, the tools the business already pays for could link together and do that work automatically, if someone set them up properly.

That is what the Ops Desk is for. We look at how your business runs day to day, pinpoint where time is being wasted, and fix it inside the tools you already have, one request at a time. The businesses we work with free up hours every week, which goes back into serving clients, quoting jobs, and growing.

Everything runs through your own request dashboard. You log what needs doing, we do it, you approve it, and you get a report every month. Simple enough for your team to run, with us in your corner month to month.

How we work

  • Fixed prices for defined results. Never hourly.
  • Your tools, your accounts. Every change lives in your systems and stays yours.
  • Plain English, written down. Every change is logged and explained.
  • Australian business, Australian hours, based in Queensland and working nationally.
